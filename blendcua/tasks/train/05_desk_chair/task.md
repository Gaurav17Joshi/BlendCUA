Only a few basic instructions (everything else is yours to plan and work out):-

Model the desk and office chair in the reference. Five steps: step 1 models the
desk, step 2 gives it materials, steps 3 and 4 do the same for the chair, and step 5
places the two together in the orientation shown.

Use gpt-image-2 to isolate the desk and the chair into separate reference images
first -- it will help get the proportions right.

sensible view that shows the object and move on. Do not spend actions hunting
an exact match to the reference framing.
