# 11_pumpkin_decor

**Autumn pumpkin decoration**

Reference: reference.png

Other plates for this task (the unedited original) are in
`reference/`: original.png.

Only a few basic instructions (everything else is yours to plan and work out):-

Model the scene in the reference: the cartoon pumpkin with its ribbed body, curled
stem and the flat black eyes, brows and smile stuck on its face; the two acorns; the
two lit pillar candles; and the maple leaves scattered on the ground. Build each
piece simply, then place them as shown -- the arrangement is part of the task.

The leaves are flat objects: an image with alpha on a rough cage mesh, get them from gpt image2
with alpha, and put them instead of modelling them.

No need for fancy lighting -- keep it simple. Same for the camera: pick a
sensible view that shows the object and move on. Do not spend actions hunting
an exact match to the reference framing.
