# 16_chocolate_cup_decor

**Hot chocolate cup decoration**

Reference: reference.png

Other plates for this task are in `reference/`: original.png (the unedited source,
which also has loose marshmallows, a peppermint swirl and chocolate squares lying on
the table and chocolate sauce dripping down the outside of the mug -- those are NOT
part of the task) and edited.png (the same as reference.png).

Only a few basic instructions (everything else is yours to plan and work out):-

Model the scene in the reference: the glossy pink cartoon mug (lathed body, curled
handle, flat black eyes with highlights, brows and smile stuck on the front); 
a mound of whipped cream on top; the chocolate sauce (to make it, make a conical mound 
then use sculpting to make such a pattern, note may have to try different brush sizes and see) 
drizzled over the cream; the dusting of cocoa on the cream; a few mini marshmallows
(white, pink, green) sitting on the cream; and a striped candy cane leaning in the cup.
Nothing lies on the table. Build each piece simply, then place them as shown.

The cream mound can be a sculpted blob, the drizzle on it a thin curve-based ribbon
lying on the cream, and the cocoa dust is a material effect. The outside of the mug is
clean glossy pink.

The table is a plain wooden plane; the background may be a blurred wood colour, no room
needed.

No need for fancy lighting -- keep it simple. Same for the camera: pick a
sensible view that shows the object and move on. Do not spend actions hunting
an exact match to the reference framing.
