# 17_watering_can

**Galvanised watering can on blue tiles**

Reference: reference.png

Other plates for this task are in `reference/`: original.jpg (the unedited
photo), edited.png (the can alone on a plain grey ground) and on_blue_tiles.png
(the same as reference.png).

Only a few basic instructions (everything else is yours to plan and work out):-

The goal is to reproduce reference.png as well as possible: the galvanised watering
can standing on a floor of glossy blue tiles.

Model the can first. (onl the can is given in image edited.png for your help, use it) 
The body is lathed, with its slanted top opening and the two
raised ribs; the long spout and the single arched handle opposite it are curves with
a bevel, joined to the body where the reference shows them. Keep the proportions --
spout length and angle, handle height -- as in the reference.

Then the floor: a plane under the can. The tiles are NOT modelled -- the blue
squares, the pale grout lines and the glossy glaze all come from a procedural
material (node-built, no image textures), with every tile the same size and the
grout straight and evenly spaced.

The can's material is procedural as well: dull galvanised steel with the spangle
pattern, the spots and the stains from nodes, can use some image textures also, be smart about it.

Match the reference's look -- the can on the tiles, its reflection in the glaze, the
plain light wall behind.
