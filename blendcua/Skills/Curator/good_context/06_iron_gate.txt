06_iron_gate   (run 06_iron_gate_run_001)
==========================================
Human judgement, written after looking at the reference photo, final_render.png, the top and
front views, a few step screenshots and (lightly) the plan. This is what a person says is
wrong, why it went wrong, and what to do about it. Severity: M = major (noticed at once),
m = minor. Classes: plan / exec / review / views (a number the single photo could not give) /
tool (the operation does not exist yet).

1. DEFECTS

#  sev  defect
1  M    The inner scrollwork is not properly mapped to the actual gate design. The curls often
        do not match the reference, are rounded out too much, and pieces of the design are
        missing altogether. It reads as "a scrolled gate", not as this gate.
2  M    The inner rods are round tubes, unlike the reference, which is flat bar.


2. BLAME

#  class                why
1  plan + exec (+ tool) The scripting part, in both planning and execution, is to blame: the
                        measurement scripts did not properly mark and identify the designs. They
                        got a few points and a somewhat idea of each curve, so what was built is
                        an approximation of the design, not the exact design. Nothing in the
                        toolkit lifts a curve out of the image properly, so the executor drew
                        spirals by hand from landmarks.
2  exec + review        The plan even says "not round hoses"; the executor made tubes anyway and
                        the review passed them.


3. FIX

#  fix
1  A tool to map curves better: crop out a region of the reference, extract the curve from it,
   build the curve, and then make a silhouette of the built curve over the reference to see how
   well they match (or something better than that; definitely some better tool than a few
   points). This would be helpful for other tasks too, anything with drawn or ornamental lines.
2  Reviewer check on the bar profile: flat rectangular section, not round.


4. OTHER
   Overall the gate reads as the reference; it is the best of the six. The frame, mid rail and
   lower bars are right. The defects are in the detail mapping of the ornament, not the
   structure.
