14_sneaker   (run 14_sneaker_run_001)
==========================================
Human judgement, written after looking at the reference photo, final_render.png, the top and
front views, a few step screenshots and (lightly) the plan. This is what a person says is
wrong, why it went wrong, and what to do about it. Severity: M = major (noticed at once),
m = minor. Classes: plan / exec / review / views (a number the single photo could not give) /
tool (the operation does not exist yet).

1. DEFECTS

#  sev  defect
1  M    The shape of the shoe is not very good; it does not follow the reference well. The part
        where the foot goes in is flat from the side, and the front near the toe is also flat:
        it stays flat and then abruptly goes down instead of curving. I know it is low poly, but
        low poly should still follow the form.
2  M    The laces are pushed toward our side and are somewhat floating above the shoe. They were
        placed to make the render look like the image rather than to be laces on a modelled shoe.
3  m    The place where the foot goes has no hole.


2. BLAME

#  class           why
1  plan + exec     Overall just bad planning and scripting in the execution of the basic
                   structure. Look at step_001/screenshots/action_05_shoe_silhouette.png: the
                   silhouette is not matching the shoe at all, a glossed-over job. It should have
                   done each part better and tried to map it properly: get the important points,
                   do a proper mapping, and a better shoe body follows. The body was half done.
2  exec (approach) The modelling focused on making it look like the image instead of making the
                   model of the object better. That is what caused the laces to be off the
                   centreline and not even touching the shoe, a huge error for a small gain.
                   Chase the image only when the task explicitly tells you to.
3  plan            The plan never asks for the collar opening as an actual hole.

   The paint work, the swoosh and the sole were good; effort was clearly spent there.


3. FIX

#  fix
1  Better planning and better scripting of the step 1 body so it is not a half done job. The
   silhouette has about 11 points in each column, which is fine (13 to 14 would be fine too),
   but they are not matched to the image properly. Match each station to the shoe outline:
   rounded toe, raised collar, proper instep. Reviewer check: silhouette overlay at heel, instep
   and toe, not only a bounding box.
2  A durable rule for planner and executor: model the object, do not chase the picture. Laces
   sit on the tongue, on the centreline, touching the upper.
3  Cut the collar opening into the upper.
