04_skull   (run 04_skull_run_001; rebuild run_002; hand tryout mem/Skull_tryout)
==========================================
Human judgement, written after looking at the reference photo, the two blueprints in the plan
folder, the step-2 screenshots of both builds, the planner's scripts folder, and the checker's
and blame agent's reports. This is what a person says is wrong, why it went wrong, and what
to do about it. Severity: M = major (noticed at once), m = minor. Classes: plan / exec /
review / views / tool.

1. DEFECTS

#  sev  defect
1  M    The head mass is a rounded box. Look at run_002 step_002 action_04: a banded loft with
        a blunt facial block, nothing like the side blueprint, which is a perfect drawing of
        the skull. The most important part of the model got the least work.
2  M    The eye sockets and nasal opening are small regular cups instead of large irregular
        plate-traced recesses in the head; a separate liner mesh stood in for the cavity.
3  M    The teeth are two uniform picket fences, and the mandible does not attach at the rami.


2. BLAME

#  class          why
1  plan + exec    The plan said "trace the front contour and side profile against the two
                  blueprint empties, then loft a 12-vertex ring through nine stations" and
                  listed the station widths. "Trace" is one word with no procedure; the width
                  table is a recipe. The executor lofted the typed numbers and never read the
                  drawing — its scripts folder for step 2 is empty, its two "blueprint check"
                  actions run the silhouette tool after the mesh exists, and five redos moved
                  numbers instead of changing method. Absolute bare minimum on the part that
                  everything else depends on.
2  views          The plates were generated by the executor in step 1, after planning, so the
                  planner never saw them. Its 50 scripts are threshold masks of the reference
                  photo (orbits, teeth, nasal opening), not one measurement of a blueprint.
3  tool           Nothing in the toolkit turned two plates into a mesh; nothing checked a
                  built outline against a plate with a number. The overlay pictures the
                  reviewer wanted never existed: Blender's Python had no Pillow, so every
                  overlay failed silently.
4  review         The step-2 reviewer measured the residual honestly (33 px and 50 px against
                  a 15 px gate) and failed the step; then one unreviewed corrective pass and the
                  step closed as exhausted, and steps 3-5 built on a head known to be wrong.


3. FIX

#  fix
1  Make the plate the geometry. Measure both plates shell-side into a station table (half-
   width and centre from the front plate, front-most and back-most point from the side plate,
   at heights chosen where the outline curves), loft rings through it, and check by drawing
   the built outline on the plate and reporting the residual per row. Done by hand in
   mem/Skull_tryout: 1.5 px front / 2.0 px side mean residual on 1024 px plates, against
   33-50 px for the runs. Now a tool: Tool_scripts/Tools_code/plate_loft.py.
2  Give the task the front and side plates (and the blueprints) as inputs, so the planner can
   measure them and write the procedure with numbers; build in the orthographic views and
   put only the final camera on the photograph's angle.
3  Features are strokes drawn inside the plate: read the orbit loops, the nasal strokes, the
   tooth arcs and the ramus edges from the drawing, cut sockets and the aperture into the
   head with an exact boolean (no liners), build the jaw as a U (body + two rami) with a mouth
   gap, and put crowns on the part of the drawn tooth band next to the bite line with an
   alveolar strip for the rest. Three steps are enough: masses, features, materials+render.
4  Blender needs Pillow (installed into its user site-packages, and run_overlay adds the path),
   or no overlay picture ever reaches an agent.


4. OTHER
   The plates themselves (front_blueprint.png, side_blueprint.png) are good — the one part of
   step 1 that was right. The dome, brow step and maxilla of the hand build read as a skull at
   181 vertices; a low-poly answer does not have to hug every bulge (the occipital curve above
   the mastoid was cut straight, 25 px off, and that is fine).
