03_monstera_pot   (run 03_monstera_pot_run_001; rebuilds run_002, run_003)
==========================================
Human judgement, written after looking at the reference photo, final_render.png of the first
build and of the two rebuilds, the checker's list and the blame agent's choices. This is what
a person says is wrong, why it went wrong, and what to do about it. Severity: M = major
(noticed at once), m = minor. Classes: plan / exec / review / views / tool.

1. DEFECTS

#  sev  defect
1  m    The stem (trunk) is a bit too thick. Everything else about it is fine.
2  m    The leaves are a bit too big, and they are all almost the same size. A little variance
        in size would make it read as a plant instead of eight copies.

   That is the whole list. The first build is about 90% there: pot, soil, roots, eight
   fenestrated leaves, colours, framing — it does all the things mostly correctly. I like
   run_001 more than run_003 (the second rebuild).


2. BLAME

#  class          why
1  exec           The Step 3 action chose the trunk core radius; nothing in the plan asked for
                  it that thick. A number the executor picked.
2  plan + exec    The plan said "change the master leaf somewhat to make other leaves (for
                  diversity)" and the executor duplicated one card at one scale. The plan did
                  not say what should vary (size first, then shape); the executor did not vary
                  anything visible.

   What the checker did with this build: 12 defects, 10 of them "major" — flat card
   geometry under the alpha (only visible in Solid shading, invisible in the render), the
   canopy too short, the trunk, the roots, floating petiole ends, edge-on collapse from two
   side views, colour and pot material. A person sees two minor things. Everything the
   checker found was true and nearly none of it changed the picture. The blame agent then
   spent the whole first round on the two things nobody can see (the hidden card rectangles
   and a replan with generated plates), and only reached the trunk in round 2. The order
   was wrong because the checker had no idea of visual weight.


3. FIX

#  fix
1  Narrow the trunk core by about a third and give it a slight bend; a one-number change in
   the Step 3 action. No new tool, no replan.
2  Scale the eight leaf instances between about 0.8 and 1.1 of the master with a couple of
   them smaller and lower, and rotate them so no two read as the same silhouette. The plan
   should name the variance ("sizes from 0.8 to 1.1, no two equal") so the executor has a
   number to hit.
3  For the harness: when a build is this close, the round should be a small R1/R2 on the
   one action, not a replan. The checker must rank by what a person sees in the final
   render (2 major + 2 minor per subject, chosen by visual weight), and the blame agent
   must pick the visible ones first.


4. OTHER
   The pot, soil, aerial roots, fenestrations and colours are fine. The rebuilt leaves of
   run_002/run_003 (real folded laminae) are geometrically better and the picture is not.
