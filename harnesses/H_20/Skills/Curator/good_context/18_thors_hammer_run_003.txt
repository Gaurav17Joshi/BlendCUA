18_thors_hammer_run_003   (run 18_thors_hammer_run_003)
==========================================
Human judgement, written after looking at the reference photo, final_render.png, the top and
front views, a few step screenshots and (lightly) the plan. This is what a person says is
wrong, why it went wrong, and what to do about it. Severity: M = major (noticed at once),
m = minor. Classes: plan / exec / review / views (a number the single photo could not give) /
tool (the operation does not exist yet).

1. DEFECTS

#  sev  defect
1  M    The head's shape is off. In our output it is a simple rectangular cuboid, whereas the
        original is much more complex: width-wise it has bigger and smaller parts, and a slant at
        the ends.
2  M    The proportions are off: the wooden handle is shorter in our output than in the
        reference, and the uplift of the ridges on the red wrap is a bit too uniform.
3  m    The texture on the head is less than in the reference.


2. BLAME

#  class          why
1  plan + exec    In the plan it made a straight front view with gpt-image-2
                  (plan/scripts/synth_plate_measurements.png) but then did not use it: the head
                  is just a badly made rectangle instead of a shape measured from that view. All
                  the measurements were done on the actual tilted reference instead of a 50-50
                  split between the two. The straight front view was made precisely because it is
                  easier to get measurements from (tracing the head, for instance), so it should
                  have been used much more.
2  plan           Once the head shape has been got from the front view, the proportions (head
                  size to handle size and so on) should have been taken from the actual image and
                  used to scale everything to fit.
3  exec           Not enough crack and wear detail put on the head.


3. FIX

#  fix
1  Plan step 1: trace the head outline (the stepped width, the slanted ends) from the front
   plate, not from the tilted photo. Durable: a planner skill that says when a straight plate
   exists, measure shape on the plate and proportions on the photo, and cite which was used for
   each number.
2  State the handle-to-head length ratio from the reference in the plan; reviewer checks the
   built ratio against it.
3  A crack coverage target for the head so "a few cracks" is not enough.


4. OTHER
   A big improvement on run_001: correct block, bands wrap around and carry rivets, handle in
   the right place.
