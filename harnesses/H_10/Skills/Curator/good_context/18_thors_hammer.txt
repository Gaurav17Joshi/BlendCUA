18_thors_hammer   (run 18_thors_hammer_run_001)
==========================================
Human judgement, written after looking at the reference photo, final_render.png, the top and
front views, a few step screenshots and (lightly) the plan. This is what a person says is
wrong, why it went wrong, and what to do about it. Severity: M = major (noticed at once),
m = minor. Classes: plan / exec / review / views (a number the single photo could not give) /
tool (the operation does not exist yet).

1. DEFECTS

#  sev  defect
1  M    One important issue: the handle is stuck to the side (the face) of the hammer head instead
        of coming out of its bottom, the way a hammer actually is. A stupid mistake.
2  M    The shape of the hammer head is not accurate at all, and the golden bands are stuck on
        weirdly. Instead of forming a loop around the head, the head was made flat and the gold
        strip was twisted to copy the look of a band going around. It copied the picture instead
        of building the object.

   The red wrap on the handle is nice.


2. BLAME

#  class          why
1  plan (views)   I am not sure exactly what to blame (I did not read the plan closely), but it is
                  definitely the plan: with a tilted reference like this, it should have used
                  gpt-image-2 to make the hammer straight, and then worked from that. Working
                  from the tilted photo is what put the handle on the face of the head.
2  plan (views)   Same as above. Because the hammer is tilted, it did not put in the effort to
                  research the real shape of the head, or the fact that the golden band goes
                  around the head.


3. FIX

#  fix
1  Get better gpt-image-2 perspectives: make the hammer upright (front, side, top), then properly
   trace the hammer head from the front plate.
2  Bands built as closed loops around the block; the handle enters the bottom face of the head.
   Reviewer check from the side view: the handle axis is perpendicular to the head's long axis.
