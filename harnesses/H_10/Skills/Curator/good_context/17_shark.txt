17_shark   (run 17_shark_run_001)
==========================================
Human judgement, written after looking at the reference photo, final_render.png, the top and
front views, a few step screenshots and (lightly) the plan. This is what a person says is
wrong, why it went wrong, and what to do about it. Severity: M = major (noticed at once),
m = minor. Classes: plan / exec / review / views (a number the single photo could not give) /
tool (the operation does not exist yet).

1. DEFECTS

#  sev  defect
1  M    The big defect is the mouth: the gum and teeth part is awkwardly sticking out on one side
        of the shark's body instead of being a mouth in the head.


2. BLAME

#  class          why
1  plan           The plan says to build it this way (separate jaw shells, gums and teeth as
                  parts) and it should not have. It should have said: make a cavity in the mouth
                  region of the head, then put the teeth inside it.


3. FIX

#  fix
1  This is something other tasks will also need, so make a tool: on a given mesh, you draw a cut
   in the side view of the object (just like texture painting or sculpting strokes are given),
   and that part is cut out from the side. Later tool calls can then expand the cut (deepen it,
   shape it). After that the teeth can be placed inside the cavity that formed. No gums at all;
   they are too hard to make well and the photo texture carries the colour anyway. The dog's
   mouth needs the same tool.


4. OTHER
   The body, the countershading and the gills are fine.
